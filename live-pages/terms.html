Terms & Conditions

Reader-led print campaign · Voting closes 30 November 2026 · Winner launches 1 December 2026

1. The campaign

Readers may vote for the First Edition, Zo Computer edition, Claude edition, or Grok edition. Voting closes at 23:59 SAST on 30 November 2026. The edition with the winning reader vote is the edition selected for the print launch on 1 December 2026.

2. Preorders

A valid print preorder reserves the winning print edition, not a guaranteed print run of the reader's preferred edition. If a reader selects a preferred edition during registration, that selection is recorded as a vote and preference only. All valid print preorders are fulfilled as the winning edition.

3. Payment and proof of payment

The current MVP accepts EFT proof of payment (POP). No payment is taken through the website, and a submitted POP remains pending until it has been reviewed. PayFast checkout will be added when the payment integration is ready. Do not pay until the correct payment instructions have been confirmed.

4. No refund and cancellation policy

Preorders are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled once submitted and accepted. This policy does not remove or limit any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer-protection law.

5. Delivery

  • Local delivery adds R100 to the order.
  • Local readers may choose PEP PAXI or The Courier Guy.
  • International delivery can be arranged through DHL. The exact rate and arrangement are confirmed separately before dispatch.
  • Print fulfilment begins after the 1 December 2026 launch and production clearance.
  • Ebook fulfilment is handled separately after POP review and confirmation.

6. Information and POP files

The preorder form collects the information needed to identify the order, review the POP, communicate campaign updates, and arrange delivery. Uploaded POP files are stored with the preorder record and are used only for payment review and fulfilment administration.

001.b — Creative Origins

Before it was a business, it was a movement.

The professional roles came later. The story actually starts in a print magazine, a club in Vincent, and a culture that insisted on making its own room. These are the projects that taught me how to build things people want to be part of.

198401 / 07

Born in Mthatha, raised in a model C school

Eastern Cape

Born Mthatha, Eastern Cape · 26 March 1984. Son of Nomhle Gwanya, teacher of financial accounting · Thozi Gwanya, farmer and rural development practitioner, and one of four siblings. Grew up in multi-racial "model C" schools on the Eastern Cape coast. Friends who couldn't quite land "Loyiso" shortened it to "Loowisoo", and the name stuck. In adulthood he took the surname "Lee" from his film mentor Spike Lee — the director who proved what a Black filmmaker could build — and folded it into Louie Lee Films.

200102 / 07

Blackbird Magazine — editor & writer

East London

Editor and contributing writer for Blackbird Magazine, an indie publication that gave voice to the underground arts and culture scene on the Eastern Cape coast. The mag was the first place I cut my teeth on long-form storytelling — laying out features, chasing copy from contributors, and learning that words on a page can pull a whole community into the same room.

2002 — 200403 / 07

Dubious Crew — producer (Funky Monkey Dubiosity sessions)

Vincent, East London

Out of Blackbird grew Dubious Crew, the production outfit behind the legendary weekly Dubiosity sessions we hosted at Funky Monkey in Vincent, East London. Spoken word, hip hop, DJ sets, jazz, theatre, and visual art thrown together in one room every week. It was a movement, a finishing school, and a creative family at the same time. Many of the careers you now see on the Eastern Cape creative scene were started or sharpened in those rooms.

2004 — 200804 / 07

Seedrack Multimedia — producer

Eastern Cape

Joined Seedrack Multimedia as a producer in the mid-2000s, working across multimedia projects, content production, and client work. First proper studio environment where I learned how to run a project from brief to delivery, manage a small team, and ship creative work against real commercial deadlines.

200905 / 07

Tango Talent — producer / artist relations

Eastern Cape

Producer and AR at Tango Talent. Talent management, bookings, artist relations. The first role where I owned the business side of the work — deals, contracts, schedules — and used that to keep building on the production and storytelling skills I'd started at Blackbird and Dubious.

201106 / 07

Izithunywa Zohlanga — album launch & live DVD

Guild Theatre, East London

Helped Izithunywa Zohlanga, a Xhosa hip hop group, release their debut album Funda — including producing a live concert DVD recorded at the Guild Theatre in East London. The whole project was about getting an indigenous-language hip hop act properly documented, properly presented, and properly seen. Album, live record, visual artefact — one project, three formats, one mission: take Xhosa hip hop seriously as art and archive.

2010 — 201407 / 07

Six short films + the Louie Lee Films catalogue

Independent · Eastern Cape

Founded Louie Lee Films in 2010 and over the next four years wrote, produced, and sometimes directed a slate of six short films — Dread Master Slashed, Sister Twinster, Unwanted, Call of the Revolution, Hear Me Out, and Entwined — alongside music videos, live DVDs, and a stream of client work. The body of work that proved the production company was real, and the seed of everything that came after.

The thread

Print. Parties. Hip hop. Xhosa language. Twenty years later, the same instinct — find the story, build the room, invite the community in.

002 — Journey

The road so far.

Over a decade of building across marketing, media, talent, and now AI — with the Eastern Cape as home base.

  1. 2022 — 2026

    Creative Director

    · ID Properties

    Led all creative output for a luxury real estate brand across photography, video, digital campaigns, and AI-assisted marketing systems. Owned brand positioning, visual identity management, and multi-platform content strategy. Relieved myself from the role in 2026 to refocus on my own creative ventures and the UCC Media work.

  2. 2020 — 2022

    Media Strategist & Producer

    · Royalty Media

    Produced and directed short-form video content, digital campaigns, and brand storytelling projects for clients across multiple industries. Built content frameworks that integrated AI tools with traditional media production pipelines.

  3. 2018 — 2020

    Founder & Lead Developer — Shayela ITMS

    · Innovo Systems & Black Colorbash Media · Ntabankulu LM

    Designed and built the Shayela Integrated Traffic Management System in partnership with Innovo Systems and Black Colorbash Media — a personal project I developed end-to-end. The system was piloted at Ntabankulu Local Municipality and improved revenue collection by more than 30%. Government procurement processes refused to extend my contract or scale it to other municipalities for a provincial and national rollout, even though the proof of concept was working. The project closed commercially — but it was the project that brought me my Mpondo wife, who has blessed me with three children so far.

  4. 2016 — 2018

    Marketing Manager

    · Kajuno Civil Construction

    Established the company's digital marketing infrastructure from the ground up. Managed corporate communications, tender documentation design, and stakeholder-facing brand materials for a growing civil engineering firm.

  5. 2013 — 2014

    Media Ministry Leader

    · Uloyiso Christian Community Church

    Led the media ministry at Uloyiso Christian Community Church in East London — producing sermon graphics, event coverage, social content, and video storytelling for a growing congregation. Volunteered alongside the Louie Lee Films client work.

  6. 2013 — 2014

    Film & Television Production (NQF 5)

    · The Media Workshop

    Completed a structured, accredited training programme in film and television production. The classroom space that turned hobbyist filmmaking into professional craft — camera, sound, edit, story.

  7. 2009 — 2013

    Talent Manager & Brand Consultant

    · Tango Talent

    Discovered new musical talent and managed end-to-end brand identity for the artists I signed — booking live performance circuit across Mthatha and East London, negotiating venue fees, handling label relations, and building visual brand assets. Promoted two artists from amateur status to commercial release. The role taught me how creative work pays: positioning, hustle, and trusting my own taste when nobody else does.

  8. 2003 — 2006

    Business Management

    · Damelin

    Studied business management through Damelin while working producer roles at Seedrack and freelance. Where I picked up the language of briefs, budgets, contracts, and client work that the rest of my career has run on.

  9. 2002

    Information Technology

    · Pretoria Tech (TUT)

    Enrolled at the Tshwane University of Technology to study Information Technology. A short stint that planted the technical seed for everything that came later — from running a studio to building AI workflows.

  10. 2001

    Matriculation

    · Hudson Park High School, East London

    Matriculated at Hudson Park High School in East London. Multi-racial Model C school, lifelong friends, the place the nickname "Louie" started sticking.

003 — FAQ

Common questions.

Answers to the most frequently asked questions about my work, rates, and availability.

What does "Creative Director & AI Storyteller" actually mean?

It means I run end-to-end creative work — concept, story, brand, visuals, and content — and I use AI to make those systems faster and more personal. You get the craft, but with a workflow that scales. I think of AI as the engine room, not the face of the work.

Are you open to full-time or contract roles?

Yes. I'm based in the Eastern Cape and open to hybrid, remote, or in-person engagements in South Africa, and remote-first roles globally. If you have a serious brief or a role that fits, use the booking page or WhatsApp.

How much do projects typically cost?

It depends on scope. A half-day shoot is in a different bracket from a full brand identity and a 6-week content system. Use the Commission form with as much detail as you have — I'll come back with a fit-score and a quote within 24 hours. Free two-minute triage, no obligation.

Can you travel for shoots?

Yes. Anywhere in South Africa is straightforward, and I take international work when the brief justifies it. Travel and accommodation are billed at cost on top of the project fee.

What's the difference between a commission, a booking, and WhatsApp?

WhatsApp is for quick chat and short questions. The booking page is for serious conversations — a 60-minute paid call where we go deep on a project or opportunity. The commission form is for when you have a real brief ready and want a written quote and timeline. Use the lightest one that fits.

How fast do you respond?

WhatsApp and the commission form get a real human reply from me within 24 hours, usually much faster. The booking page lets you pick a slot directly from my Google Calendar. If something's truly urgent, WhatsApp is always fastest.

Can I see your CV in PDF?

Yes — there's a Download CV button in the hero and About sections. It opens the modern PDF version of my résumé. The full work history is also on the Journey section of this page.

Do you take on small projects?

Sometimes. If it's a story I'd want to tell, or it benefits a community I'm already serving, yes. The Commission form has a fit-score that helps us both decide quickly. No bitterness either way.

How can I support your work?

The Support the Mission section has the donation details (Capitec, Africa Startups). You can also support by sharing projects, leaving a review, or referring me into rooms that need this kind of work. All three matter.

What is Louie Lee (the chatbot in the corner)?

Louie is my AI assistant — a small model I trained on my work, my writing, and the answers to the questions I get asked most. Ask it anything you'd ask me directly. For anything sensitive or final, it will hand you back to me on WhatsApp.

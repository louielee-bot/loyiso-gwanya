## Short description

On a farm outside East London, a missing water pump becomes the first visible crack in a story about land, loyalty, recovery, power, and the evidence it takes to tell the truth.

## Long description

Abahambe: The Last Lease is a 100-chapter literary thriller rooted in the textures of contemporary South Africa: its farms, families, churches, systems, and the quiet negotiations people make to survive. When Loyiso finds a missing pump and a crooked level, he begins building a timeline nobody can wave away. What emerges is a wider investigation into land, belonging, addiction and recovery, digital identity, public anger, and the stories powerful people tell about themselves.

## Author note

Zinzi Mlunguza writes from the intersection of place, memory, family, and public life. Abahambe is her debut novel.

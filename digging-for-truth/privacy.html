Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

This policy explains what information we collect through this site and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the information needed to fulfil it: name, delivery address, email and payment details (processed by our payment provider, not stored by us). If you message our chatbot, we may retain the text of that conversation to improve responses.

How we use it

Order information is used solely to process, fulfil and ship your order, and for the accounting and legal records we're required to keep. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing with third parties

Delivery details are shared with the carrier fulfilling your order — PEP PAXI, Courier Guy, or DHL for international orders — solely to deliver your copy. Payment details are handled directly by our payment processor.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies to remember cart contents and site preferences. No third-party advertising trackers are used.

Data retention & security

We keep order records for as long as required for accounting, tax and legal purposes, and take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete it where we're not legally required to keep it, by contacting us.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be directed to our support contact once published.
